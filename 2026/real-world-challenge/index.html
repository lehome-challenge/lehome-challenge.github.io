Real-World Challenge

Overview

The top eight participants in the Simulation Challenge will advance to the Real-World Challenge. In the Real-World Challenge, participants are required to deploy their models and conduct real-robot evaluations on site at ICRA 2026, performing garment folding tasks on clothes with different deformation states. The categories of garments used in the real-world evaluation are the same as those in the simulation challenge (i.e. long-sleeved tops, short-sleeved tops, long pants, and shorts).

Features & Deformations

The Real-World Challenge primarily focuses on evaluating a model's generalization capability when faced with different garment deformations. Therefore, for each garment, we define three distinct deformation configurations, namely:

Easy
The clothing is fully flattened.
Middle
The clothing has slight folds, but key topological points are not fully occluded.
Hard
The clothing is severely folded, and key topological points may be unidentifiable.

Important Notes

Evaluation Setting

For each garment category (4 categories total), 5 garment items (including 3 seen garments and 2 unseen garments) will be tested under each of the three initial states. Leading to 15 experimental trials per category and 60 trials per team. Each trial is subject to a time limit. The initial states of all the testing garments will be set by on-site referees to ensure fairness and impartiality in the testing of each sample.

Provided Dataset

For teams adopting Imitation Learning in the physical phase, an official folding dataset is provided. This dataset includes 5 garment (don't involve 2 unseen garment used in evaluation phase), each with three distinct initial states: 10 trajectories for the Easy state, 20 trajectories for the Middle state, and 20 trajectories for the Hard state, resulting in a total of 720 demonstration trajectories.
